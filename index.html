03 / The Journal

About Polity & Praxis

Polity & Praxis is an independent journal founded and edited by Yavnika Singh. It is a platform for independent writing, research and commentary on international relations, geopolitics, foreign policy and strategic affairs.

The journal seeks to examine the ideas, interests and political forces shaping the international order, with an emphasis on informed analysis, evidence and diverse perspectives.
